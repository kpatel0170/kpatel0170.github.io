Privacy policy

Effective September 17, 2026

Overview Terms of use

Scope

auto-reel is a private automation tool used by its owner and a limited number of known collaborators. It is not available for general public use.

Google data accessed

With the account holder’s consent, auto-reel may manage videos on authorized YouTube channels and read YouTube Analytics for those channels. It does not request Google account passwords or access unrelated Google services.

How data is used

Google data is used only to publish and manage requested YouTube content, record publishing results, and review channel performance. Analytics may guide internal content planning. Google user data is not used to train a general-purpose artificial intelligence or machine-learning model.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials and limited publishing metadata are stored on systems controlled by the project owner and protected with restricted access. Credentials are not sold, rented, used for advertising, or disclosed to data brokers.

Sharing

Google user data is not shared with third parties except where necessary to complete the Google API operations requested by the account holder or where required by law.

Retention and revocation

Credentials and related records are retained only while needed to operate the tool. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page. Revocation prevents future YouTube and Analytics operations.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the project owner using the support address shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
