Private publishing utility

auto-reel

A private command-line tool used by its owner and known collaborators to prepare and publish short-form videos to channels they control.

Privacy policy Terms of use

What the tool does

auto-reel automates parts of the content-production workflow, including video preparation, publishing, and performance review. It is not offered as a public service and has no public account-registration flow.

Google account access

The tool uses Google OAuth to access authorized YouTube channels. Access is limited to managing YouTube content and reading YouTube Analytics for those channels.

Only the project owner and explicitly authorized collaborators may use this tool.
For questions, contact the project owner using the support address shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
